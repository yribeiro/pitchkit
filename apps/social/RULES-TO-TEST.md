# Rules to test

Hypotheses about what makes a PitchKit reel travel, each with the evidence
behind it and how we'll know if it holds. They are not rules yet: promote one
to "Confirmed" only after our own numbers back it, and record the result.

Measure every test with Instagram's own insights (via vidIQ): **3-second hold
(skip rate)**, **average watch time / % watched**, **shares**, **saves** and
**comments**. Compare against our baseline below, and change one thing at a
time where we can.

## Reel 05: the first breakout (as of 8 Oct 2026, about a day after posting)

Instagram owner insights via vidIQ. Reel 05 is the 2022 World Cup final in 34 s, with music
"Movin' To The Sun" (HUGEL, Imael Angel, Ultra Naté).

| Post                          |     Plays | Skip rate |  Avg watch |  Likes | Shares | Saves | Comments |
| ----------------------------- | --------: | --------: | ---------: | -----: | -----: | ----: | -------: |
| **Reel 05 — World Cup final** | **1,336** |   **49%** | **11.5 s** | **25** |      3 |     1 |        0 |
| Reel 04 — pass networks       |       204 |     83.4% |      4.6 s |      5 |      1 |     0 |        0 |
| Spain's winner                |       182 |     62.8% |      5.4 s |      5 |      0 |     1 |        0 |
| Reel 03 — layers              |       242 |     85.4% |      2.9 s |      4 |      1 |     1 |        1 |
| Reel 02 — tracking            |       273 |     77.6% |      5.7 s |      4 |      5 |     1 |        0 |
| Reel 01 — quickstart          |       350 |     67.6% |      6.6 s |      6 |      2 |     3 |        0 |

3.8× the plays of our previous best, the first reel to keep most viewers past the opening,
and nearly double the average watch. Followers went from 18 to 22.

What we take from it (one reel, and the match, hook, song and length all changed at once, so
these are strong leads, not proof):

1. **The opening is everything.** It opened mid-match with a conflict ("France: 0 shots"),
   the clock running and a penalty already flying in. Every earlier reel opened slower or
   on a concept, and lost 63–85% of viewers.
2. **Story first, PitchKit as the medium.** A moment fans already care about (Messi,
   Mbappé, a World Cup final) reaches far beyond the analytics niche. The data supplies
   the twist; PitchKit is how the story is told, not what it's about (rule 20).
3. **Movement and sound at the right moments.** A story beat every few seconds (counter
   flip, spin, stopwatch, hat-trick, kick by kick), each with a gentle sound under the
   music. It held attention without being busy (rules 3, 13, 16, 22).
4. **The song has to match the edit.** An upbeat track with a steady pulse fitted a reel
   cut to a clock; the pacing of the music and the pacing of the story were the same
   thing (rule 21).
5. **Where it fell short:** zero comments from an open "which final next?" question (rule
   23), and only 4 follows from 1,336 plays, with PitchKit shown properly only at the end
   card (rule 24).

## Evidence sources

- **@total.fball** (Total Football, 17.7K followers), top reels analysed with
  vidIQ on 5 Oct 2026: "Inverted Fullback Overload" (3.8M plays, 14 s, silent
  2D board), "The 4-3-3 in 60 Seconds" (331K plays, 1.6K comments, 58 s 3D
  explainer with voiceover and a comment-for-e-book CTA), and "De Zerbi's
  Pressing Trap" (16K plays, slow static open) as the counter-example.
- **vidIQ outlier search** for football tactics / data reels (Oct 2026): the
  analytics reels that broke out were almost all music-only with a rhythmic beat
  from the first frame.
- **Hook research** (Opus, inro, Klap, CapCut, Sovran, GoFaceless, Oct 2026):
  viewers decide in ~3 s; visual hooks beat text hooks; a pattern break every
  8–12 s cut drop-off from 22% to 8% in one 50-video analysis.
- **Four-beat hook formulas** (another creator's reel, screenshot shared on
  9 Oct 2026; creator not recorded): five script rows, one beat per column.
  Results first → What you did → Learn → Step. A → B → C → Stay. Common belief
  → Contradict → Stay → Why. Better way → Best way → Rehook → Watching. Won't
  work → Unless → (rest hidden). Logged in Notion under "Growth patterns —
  social content".
